The process

Before you ask

Common questions from parents.

Is the first class really free?+
Yes — one full trial class, with no payment details required, so you can judge the fit before committing to anything.
Do you offer in-home tutors outside India?+
In-home tutoring is currently available across major Indian cities. Outside India, all 1‑on‑1 classes run over live video through your portal.
Can I switch tutors if it's not working?+
Absolutely — a free rematch is available within the first two weeks of any plan, no questions asked.
Where do I manage my classes and payments?+
Everything — scheduling, recordings, invoices and progress reports — lives in your student portal, linked throughout this site.
How do the sale offers get applied?+
Current offers are pre-linked from the Batches & Offers section above — the discount code or credit is applied automatically at checkout in the portal.
